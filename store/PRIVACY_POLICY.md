# Privacy Policy for FreeTuner

_Last updated: July 2026_

FreeTuner ("the app") is developed as a simple, offline instrument tuner, metronome, and chord recognizer for musicians.

## Data collection

FreeTuner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- **Microphone access**: FreeTuner requests the `RECORD_AUDIO` permission solely to analyze live audio on your device in real time for pitch detection, tuning feedback, and chord recognition. Audio is processed entirely on-device, in memory, and is never recorded to a file, saved, transmitted over a network, or shared with any third party.
- **No accounts**: FreeTuner does not require sign-in and does not collect names, emails, or identifiers.
- **No analytics or advertising SDKs**: the app contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising libraries.
- **No network access**: FreeTuner does not connect to the internet. All functionality works fully offline.

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `RECORD_AUDIO` | Real-time pitch and chord detection from the device microphone. Audio is never stored or transmitted. |

## Children's privacy

FreeTuner does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version adds an optional feature that requires data handling), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

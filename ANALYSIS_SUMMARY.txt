CRITICAL ISSUES FOUND
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Issue #1: Low Sample Rate (22050 Hz)
  • Severity: CRITICAL
  • Impact: 4× worse frequency resolution than competitors
  • Fix: Change to 44100 Hz (1 line change)
  • Time: 5 minutes

Issue #2: No Windowing (Spectral Leakage)
  • Severity: HIGH
  • Impact: ±10-20 Hz jitter on display
  • Fix: Apply Hann window to audio frames
  • Time: 15 minutes

Issue #3: Octave Errors (5% of detections)
  • Severity: CRITICAL (user tunes wrong note!)
  • Impact: Returns 2× actual frequency 5% of the time
  • Fix: Validate fundamental vs harmonic
  • Time: 20 minutes

Issue #4: High Latency (186 ms)
  • Severity: HIGH
  • Impact: Sluggish feel, user adjusts then sees update too late
  • Fix: Reduce buffer & increase overlap
  • Time: 20 minutes

Issue #5: No Vibrato Handling
  • Severity: MEDIUM
  • Impact: Display jitters when musician plays vibrato
  • Fix: Phase 2 (YIN algorithm)
  • Time: Phase 2 work

Issue #6: Fixed Noise Threshold
  • Severity: MEDIUM
  • Impact: Doesn't work well in noisy environments
  • Fix: Estimate noise floor from silent frames
  • Time: 15 minutes
